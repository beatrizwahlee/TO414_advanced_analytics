# 📊 TO414 -- Advanced Analytics

### Ross School of Business \| University of Michigan

## 🔎 About the Course

This repository contains projects, notebooks, and assessments completed
for **TO414 -- Advanced Analytics** at the Ross School of Business
(University of Michigan).

The course focuses on applying data analytics and machine learning
techniques to solve real-world business problems. Emphasis is placed on:

-   Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)\
-   Regression modeling\
-   Classification models\
-   Model evaluation & comparison\
-   Ensemble methods\
-   Business interpretation of predictive results

All work was implemented primarily in **R**, using RMarkdown for
structured analytical reporting.
